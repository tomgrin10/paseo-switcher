Type-to-find works when you typed on the wrong keyboard layout, and the arrows are back next to the sidebar button on Paseo 0.17.

### New
- Type-to-find finds the workspace even when you typed on the wrong keyboard layout, for example "smart session" typed on Hebrew. Matches for what you typed stay first, and a quiet "Showing results for …" line says when the search was converted. Any layout works on a physical keyboard; pasted text and phone keyboards fall back to Hebrew and Russian.

### Fixed
- On Paseo 0.17 the back and forward arrows sit next to the sidebar button again, instead of on a row of their own.
