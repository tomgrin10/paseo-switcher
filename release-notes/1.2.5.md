Ctrl+Tab looks cleaner: previews survive a reload, the strip shows whole cards, and list rows are tidier.

### Improved
- The strip shows only whole cards. When more workspaces are open than fit, the next card peeks in at the edge and a soft fade marks the side that scrolls.
- List rows drop the extra badge and separate their details with a quiet dot, so names are easier to scan.

### Fixed
- Workspace previews stay after you reload Paseo instead of coming back blank. They are kept in the browser, capped in size, and saved without slowing the switcher down.
